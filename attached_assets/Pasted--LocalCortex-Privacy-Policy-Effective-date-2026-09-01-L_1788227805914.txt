
# LocalCortex Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-09-01

LocalCortex ("the app", "we", "us") is a task and effort manager for macOS and
iOS, developed by Shengchen Liu. This policy explains what
information the app handles and what we do — and, just as importantly, what we
never do — with it.

**The short version: LocalCortex has no user accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no tracking. We do not collect your personal information.
Your data stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account.**

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only:

- **Your efforts, tasks, templates, and agent settings** in the app's local
  database.
- **App preferences** (appearance, view filters) and **folder locations** you
  link the app to.
- A **local crash log** (written only if the app crashes, to help diagnose the
  problem). This file never leaves your device unless you choose to send it to
  us.

## Information synced through your iCloud account

If you enable sync, the app's structured data (efforts, tasks, templates, and
agent settings) syncs between your devices through **your own iCloud account**
using Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is stored in your iCloud and
is protected by your Apple ID. **We have no access to your iCloud data.** iCloud
is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Your files

LocalCortex links to folders that are already on your disk. **The app never
uploads your files anywhere.** If you place your folders in a sync service
(such as iCloud Drive, Dropbox, or Google Drive), those files are handled
entirely by that service under its own terms and privacy policy. We never
receive copies.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by **Apple** through the App Store. We
never see your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Your subscription status is
stored on your device.

## Push notifications

The app may register for silent push notifications with Apple's push service
solely to keep your devices' data up to date. No promotional notifications are
sent, and no third-party server is involved.

## What we never do

- We do not require an account, and we never ask for your name, email, or
  contact details through the app.
- We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or diagnostics from your
  device.
- We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you
  across apps or websites.
- We do not sell, rent, or share personal information — we do not have any to
  share.
- We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is
  not directed at children.

## Crash reports

Crash reports are generated by Apple's operating system. They are shared with
Apple (and, in aggregate, with developers) only if you have enabled diagnostic
sharing in your device settings. That data is governed by Apple's privacy
policy, not this one.

## Deleting your data

Delete efforts within the app, remove the app's data from iCloud via your
device's iCloud settings, or uninstall the app — your data is then gone from
the places it existed. Since we never collected anything, there is nothing
left on our side to delete.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this page and note the
change in the app's release notes. The effective date above will always show
when this policy was last updated.
